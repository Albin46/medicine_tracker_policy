Privacy Policy

Medicine Tracker - Medicine Reminder & Tracker

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Medicine Tracker stores your medicine information only on your phone. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not collect your personal or health data on any server, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

Medicine Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a medicine reminder and tracking app developed by [Your Name / Company Name]. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at [your-email@example.com].

2. Information the app handles

To work as a reminder app, Medicine Tracker lets you enter the following details. All of them are saved locally on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Medicine names, doses, purpose notes and food timing (before / after food)
  • Reminder times, start dates, course length and how often you take each medicine
  • Your daily "taken" records, so the app can show your progress
  • Your meal-time settings (breakfast, lunch, dinner, bedtime)
  • Doctor details and prescription photos, if you choose to add them

This information may relate to your health. We treat it as sensitive and keep it on your device only.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email address or location.
  • We do not require sign-up or login.
  • We do not use advertising and we do not use advertising IDs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot normally read it. Because nothing is uploaded, we cannot see, recover or delete your data for you. Please keep your phone secure with a screen lock.

If your phone uses Android's cloud backup, Google may back up app data as part of your device backup. That is controlled by your Google account and device settings, not by us.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. The app does not contain third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

7. Your choices and control

  • Edit or delete: you can edit or delete any medicine inside the app at any time.
  • Delete everything: clear the app's storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app. This removes all data saved by the app.
  • Permissions: you can withdraw any permission in your phone settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Because no data leaves the device, a parent or guardian can manage a child's medicine reminders on the parent's own phone.

9. Medical disclaimer

Medicine Tracker is a reminder and tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. Reminders may be delayed or missed because of phone settings, battery saving modes or system limits, so do not rely on the app alone for critical medication.
